Advanced Voxel - Touch/UI + Ngu Kiem Fix

Changes:
1. Removed/hidden legacy sword skill buttons and panels so they cannot overlap the new 20-skill bar.
2. Repositioned Kho/Chế tạo quick buttons to the top-right.
3. Made the 20-skill bar compact, horizontally scrollable, and reserved the right side for the jump button.
4. Ngự Kiếm now adapts its base ride height to higher terrain, preventing the player from sinking through raised blocks.
5. While Ngự Kiếm is active, Jump gives a short upward lift (~1 block) instead of requiring grounded state.
6. Returning to lower terrain restores the normal activation height (plus the temporary jump arc).
